# MicFlow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

MicFlow is a free Mac app made by Abdullah Al Mamun. This page explains, in plain words, what MicFlow
stores, where, and who can see it.

## What stays on your Mac

- Your dictation history, stats, screenshots and screen recordings.
- Your settings, dictionary words, mix-ups and instructions.
- Your API keys, kept in the Mac's Keychain.

None of this leaves your Mac unless you use one of the features below.

## Voice typing

- When you dictate, your voice is sent to a cloud speech-recognition service to turn it into text,
  using **your own API key**. The cleaned-up version of your text is made the same way.
- On a free plan, the provider of that service may use what you send to improve their products, and
  people may review it. Don't dictate passwords or very private information.
- MicFlow itself doesn't keep a copy of your voice or text anywhere except on your Mac.

## Signing in (optional)

You can use MicFlow without an account. If you choose **Sign in with Google**:

- MicFlow receives your **name, email address and profile photo** from Google. It doesn't get your
  Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
- Your **instructions, dictionary words, mix-ups and API key** are saved to your account, so they come
  back after a reinstall or on another Mac. Only your own sign-in can read them back. The app's maker
  runs the database and could technically see stored data.

## Feature requests and votes

- Requests, pictures and comments you post are **public**: everyone using MicFlow can see them, along
  with your name and photo.
- To vote without signing in, MicFlow creates an anonymous ID for your Mac. Votes are counted, but
  they aren't shown with any name.

## Screenshot links (optional)

If you add your own ImgBB key, screenshots you choose to upload go to **your** ImgBB account and are
visible to anyone with the link. ImgBB's own privacy policy applies to those images.

## What MicFlow never does

- No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data.
- No access to your files, messages or other apps beyond what each feature needs.

## Where the data is kept

Account data and feature requests are kept in a hosted database (Supabase), protected so each person
can only read their own private data.

## Deleting your data

- To remove everything from your Mac, delete MicFlow and its folder
  `~/Library/Application Support/MicFlow`.
- To delete your account and everything saved to it, send a message using the contacts below, and
  it will be deleted.
