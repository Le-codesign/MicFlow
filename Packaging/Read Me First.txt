MICFLOW
A helper for your Mac. Talk instead of type, take pictures and videos of
your screen, make your microphone and speakers louder, and make your mouse
and keyboard nicer to use.
Works on any Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) with macOS 13 Ventura or newer.


=====================================================================
 INSTALL  (about 5 minutes, your Mac restarts twice)
=====================================================================

STEP 1. Install the first sound helper
   - Double-click "1 Install BlackHole 2ch".
   - Click Continue, Agree and Install, and type your Mac password.
   - At the end, click Restart.
   Why? MicFlow needs a small "hidden cable" inside your Mac to pass your
   louder voice to apps like Zoom. This installs that cable.

STEP 2. Install the second sound helper
   - After your Mac restarts, open this download (the .dmg file) again.
   - Double-click "2 Install BlackHole 16ch" and install it the same way.
   - At the end, click Restart.
   Why? This second cable is for making your Mac's own sound louder.

STEP 3. Install MicFlow
   - Open this download again.
   - Drag "MicFlow" onto the "Applications" folder next to it.

STEP 4. Open MicFlow the first time (important!)
   Your Mac protects you from apps it doesn't know yet, so the first time
   it stops MicFlow. This is normal.
   - Open your Applications folder and double-click "MicFlow".
   - When the warning appears, click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   - Scroll down. Next to "MicFlow was blocked..." click "Open Anyway".
   - Type your password, then click "Open Anyway" again.
   You only do this once.

STEP 5. Say yes when MicFlow asks for permission
   See "WHY MICFLOW ASKS FOR PERMISSION" below.

Done! MicFlow now has an icon in your Dock (the row of apps at the bottom
of the screen) and a small microphone icon in the menu bar (the strip at
the very top of the screen, near the clock). It also starts by itself
when you log in; you can change that in General.


=====================================================================
 WHERE TO FIND EVERYTHING
=====================================================================

THE HOME WINDOW
   Open MicFlow like any app: click it in the Dock, in Launchpad, or
   press Command + Space, type MicFlow and press Return.
   The left side has a button for each part: Home, Dictionary, Sound,
   Voice, Mouse, Keyboard, Figma, Capture and General.

THE SMALL MENU BAR PANEL
   Click the microphone icon at the top of the screen. Same settings,
   in a small panel.

ON AND OFF
   Every feature has its own switch. Pink means on.

CLOSING AND QUITTING
   Closing the window doesn't stop MicFlow; it keeps helping in the
   background. To stop it completely, choose Quit in the panel, or press
   Command + Q while the window is open. Your Mac's sound, mouse and
   keyboard go back to normal.


=====================================================================
 EVERY FEATURE, EXPLAINED SIMPLY
=====================================================================

--- HOME: your voice typing scoreboard -------------------------------

   Like a step counter, but for your voice.
   - Words today: how many words you said today.
   - Calendar: every day you used voice typing turns pink.
   - Weekly chart: one bar per day; today's bar is yellow.
   - Average speed: words per minute while speaking. (Most people type
     about 40 a minute but speak about 150, which is why talking is
     faster.)
   - Total words, streak (days in a row) and time saved.
   - Languages: how much English and how much Bengali you spoke.
   - Recent dictations: everything you said, as colorful cards. Click a
     card to copy its words again, or search for something you said.
   Your history stays only on your Mac.

--- VOICE: talk instead of type --------------------------------------

   1. Click where you want the words: an email, a chat, anywhere.
   2. HOLD your voice key (at first: the Right Control key).
   3. TALK normally. A glowing pill with a moving wave appears at the
      bottom of the screen; your words show above it as you speak.
   4. LET GO. A moment later your words are typed for you.

   Example: you hold the key and say "Hi Sara, the meeting moved to
   Thursday." When you let go, that sentence appears in your email.

   - Speak English, Bengali, or both mixed in one sentence.
   - "Um" and "uh" are removed, and full stops and commas are added.
   - Your words are also copied, so you can paste them again (Cmd + V).
   - To change the key: Voice > Keys, click the key's button, then press
     the key you want.

   FIRST-TIME SETUP: open MicFlow > Voice, turn on Voice typing, and
   follow the steps on that page.

--- DICTIONARY: teach MicFlow your words -----------------------------

   MY WORDS
   Names and brands you want spelled your way.
   Example: add "Figma" once, and it's always written with a capital F.

   MIX-UPS
   Words that come out wrong because of how you say them.
   Example: you say "world" but it writes "word". Add "word -> world",
   and MicFlow fixes it only when the sentence really means "world":
   "The whole world is watching" is fixed; "spell this word" stays.

--- CAPTURE: pictures and videos of your screen ----------------------

   Each tool has a shortcut: hold Option + Shift + Command and press a
   number.

   Capture Area (4)      Draw a box; MicFlow saves a picture of it. One
                         click takes the whole screen. Esc cancels.
                         Example: saving just one chart from a web page.
   Previous Area (8)     The same box as last time.
   Fullscreen (3)        The whole screen.
   Capture Window (6)    Click a window to capture only that window.
   Self-Timer (9)        Counts down, then takes a picture. Example: open
                         a menu first, then let the timer capture it.
   Capture Text (7)      Draw a box over text, even text in a picture,
                         and the words are copied for you to paste.
                         Example: copying a phone number from a photo.
   Record Screen (5)     A video of a box or the whole screen. Click the
                         red timer at the top of the screen to stop.

   After each capture, a small preview appears in the bottom-left
   corner. From there you can:
   - Mark it up: arrows, boxes, circles, text, numbered dots, or blur
     private things like a password.
   - Copy it, copy its text, upload it to your GitHub account and get a
     link to share, or show it in Finder.
   Everything is saved in Pictures > MicFlow Captures.

--- SOUND: louder voice, louder speakers -----------------------------

   MICROPHONE BOOST (make your voice louder)
   Example: on Zoom, people say you're too quiet.
   - Turn on Microphone and pick the microphone you talk into.
   - Move "Boost" to the right.
   - In Zoom, Teams, Discord, Google Meet, FaceTime, etc., choose
     "BlackHole 2ch" as the microphone. That's your louder voice.
   - The bar shows how loud you are. Cream or yellow is good; pink means
     it's at the limit, and MicFlow stops it from crackling.

   SPEAKER BOOST (make your Mac louder)
   Example: a video is too quiet even at full volume.
   - Turn on Speakers, pick where you listen, and move "Boost" up.
   - Your volume keys still work. With headphones, start low!

--- MOUSE: nicer to use ----------------------------------------------

   Tracking speed    The pointer moves faster than macOS's fastest
                     setting. Good for big screens.
   Scroll distance   How far a page moves each time you scroll.
   Smooth scrolling  Pages glide gently instead of jumping.
   Swipe gestures    (Apple Magic Mouse) Swipe one finger left or right
                     to move between your desktops (Spaces).
   Mouse buttons     (A regular mouse with side buttons) Use the side
                     buttons to move between desktops.
   Middle click      (Apple Magic Mouse) Press the middle of the mouse.
                     Example: middle-click a link to open a new tab.

--- KEYBOARD: make keys act differently ------------------------------

   On each row, pick the key you press on the left and what it should
   do on the right. Example: on a Windows keyboard, make the Windows key
   act like Command, or make F11 turn the volume down. Click
   "Add remap" for more rows.

--- FIGMA: zoom with a swipe -----------------------------------------

   In the Figma desktop app, swipe up or down on the mouse to zoom, with
   no extra key. "Zoom per swipe" sets how much each swipe zooms.


=====================================================================
 WHY MICFLOW ASKS FOR PERMISSION
=====================================================================

Your Mac keeps you safe by asking before an app can do certain things.

MICROPHONE
   So MicFlow can hear you for voice typing and the microphone boost.

ACCESSIBILITY
   So MicFlow can type your words for you and change how your mouse and
   keyboard behave. Without it, MicFlow hears you but can't put the
   words in your email.
   Turn it on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.

SCREEN RECORDING
   So MicFlow can take pictures and videos of your screen. Without it,
   screenshots show only the desktop background.
   Turn it on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System
   Audio Recording, then quit and reopen MicFlow.


=====================================================================
 YOUR PRIVACY
=====================================================================

- Voice typing sends what you say to a secure cloud speech service to
  turn it into text. Please don't dictate passwords or very private
  information. The Voice page explains the details.
- Everything else stays on your Mac: your history, stats, screenshots,
  recordings, and text read from pictures.
- Your keys and passwords for connected services are kept in the Mac's
  secure Keychain.


=====================================================================
 IF SOMETHING DOESN'T WORK
=====================================================================

"BlackHole ... isn't installed" in red
   -> Run Step 1 or Step 2 again, then restart your Mac.

People can't hear your boosted voice on a call
   -> In the call app's settings, choose "BlackHole 2ch" as the
      microphone.
   -> In System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, make sure
      MicFlow is on.

No sound from the speakers or headphones
   -> In Sound > Speakers, pick the device you're listening on.

Voice typing hears you but doesn't type
   -> Turn MicFlow on in System Settings > Privacy & Security >
      Accessibility. If it's already on, turn it off and on again.

Voice typing shows an error
   -> Open the Voice page and read the message there; it says what to
      fix.

A Mouse, Keyboard or Figma feature does nothing
   -> Check that its switch is on (pink), and read the text under it.
   -> "No Magic Mouse found": make sure the mouse is on and connected.

Swipe gestures jump two desktops at once
   -> Another app (like BetterTouchTool) also reacts to the swipe. Turn
      the same gesture off there.

Screenshots show only the desktop background
   -> Turn on MicFlow in Screen & System Audio Recording (see above),
      then quit and reopen MicFlow.

Key remaps don't work
   -> If Karabiner-Elements is installed, quit or remove it.

The pointer is too fast
   -> Move Mouse > Tracking speed back to the left, or turn it off.

Bluetooth headphones sound a bit flat
   -> That's normal while they're also used as the microphone (they
      switch to "call mode"). It happens with every app.


=====================================================================
 UNINSTALL
=====================================================================

1. Quit MicFlow (menu bar icon > Quit).
2. Drag "MicFlow" from Applications to the Trash.
3. To remove the sound helpers too: in Finder choose Go > Go to Folder,
   type  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL  and drag "BlackHole2ch.driver" and
   "BlackHole16ch.driver" to the Trash. Then restart your Mac.
